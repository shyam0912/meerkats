The exact name **2-methylbutane** is derived by applying the source's single-branch naming rules to its C5H12 branched example. The source's worked p. 10 example is 2-methylpentane; we do not misattribute that worked name to the smaller molecule. This reference lesson deliberately reuses the smaller pair across the sequence.

## Scope and rights boundary

Curriculum facts are source-grounded; wording, layout, SVG molecular representations, candidate-path controls, name cards, and interaction sequence are original Meerkats work. No page scans, textbook artwork, characters, tables, or long passages are redistributed. The owner-supplied PDF remains outside the application. `textbook-reference` indicates provenance, not a license assertion.

Only acyclic saturated carbon chains with a single methyl branch are taught here. This is not the whole unit, a universal molecular editor, a reaction animation, or an exhaustive list of pentane isomers. The third pentane isomer and advanced naming/isomer categories are intentionally deferred. Arrangement switching compares structures; it does not model a chemical transformation or track individual hydrogen atoms.
